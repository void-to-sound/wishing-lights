Ornament color transfer

Upload three photographs. Each one lends five colors to the glass. Switch ornaments to compare the milky V1 ball with the faceted V2 crystal. The metal cap, bright reflections, dark shadows, and empty background stay as they are.

Look for real glass: reflections should stay bright, the cap should stay silver, and three very different palettes should still read as the same ornament.
